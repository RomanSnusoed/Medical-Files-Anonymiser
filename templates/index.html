A testable document-processing pipeline

1. Extract & digitise

Extract text from PDF pages and apply OCR to image-only pages; scanned output requires human review.

2. Detect languages

Auto-detect whether the source is English, German, or Russian before translating to English for the redaction engine.

3. Clean personally identifiable data

Combine Presidio, multilingual transformers, and regex sweeps to redact names, emails, patient identifiers, and contextual clues across the document, with known false-negative and false-positive risks.

4. Re-translate & package

Deliver the sanitised result in your chosen language, produce a text-only PDF for manual review, and optionally request an external explanation with explicit consent.
